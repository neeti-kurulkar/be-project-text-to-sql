# Financial Analysis Insights

**Question:** What are the key profitability metrics for 2024?

**Date:** 2025-10-10 22:27:55

---

## Executive Summary
Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) reported strong profitability metrics for 2024, with a net profit margin of 16.60% and an operating profit margin of 23.68%. These metrics indicate a healthy financial performance for the company in 2024.

## Key Findings
* Net Profit Margin for 2024 was 16.60%, which is in line with the average across years.
* Operating Profit Margin for 2024 was 23.68%, indicating a strong operational performance.
* Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) was 27.83% in 2024, suggesting efficient use of capital.
* Return on Net Worth (RONW) was 20.06% in 2024, indicating a good return on shareholder equity.
* Return on Assets Excluding Revaluations was 218.86% in 2024, highlighting the company's ability to generate profits from its assets.

## Analysis
The profitability metrics for 2024 indicate a stable financial performance for HUL. The net profit margin and operating profit margin are in line with the average across years, suggesting a consistent performance. The high ROCE and RONW indicate that the company is generating strong returns on its capital and shareholder equity. The return on assets excluding revaluations is also high, suggesting that the company is able to generate significant profits from its assets.

The fact that all the profitability metrics are in line with the average across years suggests that the company has a stable financial performance. However, it also indicates that there may not be significant improvements in profitability in 2024 compared to previous years.

## Implications
* The strong profitability metrics suggest that HUL can continue to invest in its business and return value to shareholders.
* The stable financial performance indicates that the company can weather any potential economic downturns or industry disruptions.
* The high ROCE and RONW suggest that the company should continue to focus on efficient use of capital and generating strong returns on shareholder equity to drive long-term growth and profitability.
