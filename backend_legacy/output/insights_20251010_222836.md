# Financial Analysis Insights

**Question:** How has total assets grown from 2021 to 2025?

**Date:** 2025-10-10 22:28:37

---

## Executive Summary
Hindustan Unilever Limited's (HUL) total assets have grown from 68,757 INR Crores in 2021 to 79,880 INR Crores in 2025, representing a cumulative growth of 16.18% over the four-year period. This growth indicates a steady expansion of the company's asset base, with varying year-over-year (YoY) increases.

## Key Findings
* Total assets grew by 2.56% in 2022, 3.64% in 2023, 7.40% in 2024, and 1.76% in 2025, with the highest YoY growth observed in 2024.
* The company's asset base increased by 14,123 INR Crores from 2021 to 2025, with an average annual growth of 3.54%.
* The growth multiple, which compares the total assets in 2025 to those in 2021, stands at 1.16, indicating a moderate expansion of the company's asset base.

## Analysis
The data shows a generally upward trend in HUL's total assets, with the most significant YoY growth observed in 2024 at 7.40%. This increase may be attributed to various factors such as investments in new projects, acquisitions, or expansion of existing operations. The slower growth in 2025, at 1.76%, could be a result of market saturation, increased competition, or other external factors. The cumulative growth of 16.18% over the four-year period suggests that the company has been able to maintain a steady pace of expansion.

The growth in total assets can be seen as a positive indicator of the company's ability to invest in its operations and expand its business. However, it is essential to consider other factors such as profitability, debt levels, and return on investment to get a comprehensive view of the company's financial health.

## Implications
* The steady growth in total assets suggests that HUL is well-positioned to continue investing in its operations and expanding its business, which could lead to increased revenue and profitability.
* The varying YoY growth rates indicate that the company may need to adapt to changing market conditions and adjust its investment strategies accordingly.
* The moderate growth multiple of 1.16 implies that HUL may need to focus on optimizing its asset utilization and improving its return on investment to drive further growth and increase shareholder value.
